  1. Download the template and fill it in (or export your data to the same columns from Excel as CSV).
  2. Patients are matched by Patient number. Choices (such as Severity) use the same wording as the incident form.
  3. Put several choices in one cell with |, for example Verbal Aggression|Threatening Behaviour. Dates can be 2026-03-05 or 05/03/2026 (day first).

Nothing is saved until you press Import. Up to 5000 rows per file.
